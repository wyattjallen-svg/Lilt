Lilt 0.9 brings the sleeping tabs, memory view and start-over from 0.8, plus a universal font, a tab overview, a reading list, a steadier media player and a crash log.

**Download `Lilt-v0.9.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### Look

- **Universal font** (Ctrl+K → Universal font for pages): sets the font for the pages you browse too, not just Lilt's own bars and menus. Turn it off to go back to each site's own font.

### Panel and player

- **The panel holds widgets, not pages.** The player, the visualizer and a popped-out video can go in the panel. Whole pages can't: drag the pop-out video's frame onto the panel, and it plays on the side while you browse. Right-click a tab → Move the video to the panel is the same thing.
- The panel's width and the visualizer's height are both draggable.
- The track line no longer stops at a short clip's length. Lilt follows the long track when a short sound plays alongside it.
- The visualizer retries page sound when a page has no audio yet, instead of staying blank.

### Easier

- **Show all tabs** (Ctrl+K): the tabs in this workspace as cards. Click one to go there. Sleeping tabs show their title without waking up.
- **Add this page to the reading list** (Ctrl+K): the page goes into a "Reading list" bookmarks folder.

### Steadier

- Errors on the UI thread are logged and shown as a notice. Lilt keeps running unless errors keep coming, and then it stops. The details are in the logs folder (Ctrl+K → Open data folder → logs).
- If Lilt didn't close properly last time, it says so and offers the log.
- Developer switches (`--user-data`, `--run`, `--browser-args` and the rest) only work when `LILT_DEV=1` is set. A shortcut or another app can't change the engine or run commands.

Everything in Lilt 0.8's notes is included, including the start-over wipe, sleeping tabs and the memory list.
