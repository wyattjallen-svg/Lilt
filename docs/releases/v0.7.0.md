Lilt 0.7 adds the panel, site colors and a storage tidy-up that never signs you out.

**Download `Lilt-v0.7.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### The panel (F4)

A column beside the page for the tabs you want to keep around: Spotify, YouTube, a chat, notes, anything.

- **Drag a tab into it** (a drop target appears while you drag), right-click a tab → Move to the panel, or use the panel's own menu → "Put the current tab here" or "Open an address here…". The page moves as it is: music keeps playing and you stay signed in.
- Keep several sites there; the buttons along its top switch between them (drag to reorder, right-click to mute, move back to the tabs or remove).
- **The visualizer and the now-playing player dock under it**, so Spotify plays at the side with its visualizer right below while you browse. Leave either out and it floats over the page as before.
- It keeps playing while you browse, switch workspaces, or hide the panel (F4, the ✕ in its corner, the toolbar button, or the panel button at the bottom of the sidebar).
- **Make it yours**: left or right of the page, any width (drag its edge), and the order of what's in it (Customize → Layout → Panel, or its menu). Saved with your session.

### Site colors

The toolbar takes on the color of the site you're on (its own theme color, or the color along the top of the page), and the sidebar does too, more gently. It never gets so strong that text becomes hard to read. Set how strong each one is in Customize → Look → Site colors (0 turns it off).

### Storage that stays small, sign-ins that stay put

Customize → Privacy → Storage shows what sites keep on your PC (cached files, offline copies, site data) and has:

- **A cache limit** (300 MB to start; takes effect after a restart). Sites' offline copies are emptied when they grow past it too.
- **Empty caches now**: cached files and offline copies only. Nothing that keeps you signed in.
- **Tidy up sites you've stopped using** (after 90 days, or what you choose): what they stored and their readable cookies go, and their sign-in cookies stay, so you're still signed in if you go back. Bookmarked sites and open tabs are never touched, and you can list sites to leave alone (or Ctrl+K → "Never tidy up this site"). If you'd rather be signed out of those sites too, there's a box for that.

Lilt checks a minute after starting and every six hours after that, in the background.

### Also

- Everything new is in the command palette (Ctrl+K): the panel, its side, docking the visualizer or player, site colors, emptying caches, tidy-up on or off.
- Shortcuts: F4 shows or hides the panel; "Move this tab to the panel" has no key yet, so give it one in Customize → Keys if you like.
- A panel button for the toolbar: it's there by default on a fresh `theme.json`; if you've customized yours, tick "Panel button" in Customize → Layout → Toolbar. The sidebar's bottom row has one either way.
- A `theme.json` you never edited is updated to the new default, so the new settings are written out with their explanations; an edited one keeps everything and uses the defaults for what's new.
