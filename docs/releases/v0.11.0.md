Lilt 0.11 makes Now playing work with Spotify and players like it, turns the panel into a proper column for what's playing, and writes down what the visualizer hears so a silent one can be tracked down.

**Download `Lilt-v0.11.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### Now playing with Spotify

- Spotify's web player plays through a media element it never puts in the page, so Lilt couldn't see it. It saw whatever short sound was in the page instead, which is where the 7-second track line came from. Now Lilt reads the page's media session: title, artist and artwork. Windows' media controls supply whether it's playing, how far in it is and how long the track is.
- Play, pause, seeking on the track line, next and previous work for these players too, through Windows' media controls.
- When a track changes, the player catches up within a few seconds, and as soon as sound starts or stops.

### The panel, as a column

- **The player becomes a column when it's docked:** artwork as wide as the panel, then the title (two lines if it needs them), the time, and the controls centered underneath. Floating, it stays the compact strip.
- **The visualizer fills whatever height is left** and shows whenever something plays while it's docked, not only on music sites.
- **A video in the panel keeps its 16:9 shape** at the top, and the player under it drops the artwork, since the video is already showing.
- New order: videos, then the player, then the visualizer. It's still yours to change in Customize → Layout.
- The visualizer's separate height handle in the panel is gone, since it now takes the space it's given. Floating, it still resizes from its corner.

### Finding out why the visualizer is quiet

- About six seconds after the visualizer starts with something playing, Lilt writes down what it heard, at `about:logs` (filter: Info or Warnings):
  - How much sound Windows passed it (packets, how many were silent, the loudest moment).
  - What the page itself gave.
  - Which site was playing.
- If it heard nothing, that line says why, so you can copy it to me.

### Also

- Notices rise above a floating player or visualizer parked at the bottom of the page instead of covering its controls.
