Lilt 0.6 is the customization release, plus the things that make a browser easy to live in: workspaces, a bookmark manager, mouse gestures and site permissions.

**Download `Lilt-v0.6.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### Fixed

- **The home tab's "None" background now sticks.** Two things could bring the hills (or your image) back: picking a theme preset replaced your background with the preset's, and the "None" choice didn't always register. Presets now keep your home background, and None always applies.

### New

- **See-through websites** (Customize → Look, or Ctrl+K "See-through websites"). The window's Mica or Acrylic shows behind web pages: Lilt keeps a share of each page's own color (you choose how much) and clears the page's large solid backgrounds. It's off by default because some sites get harder to read; turn it off for one site from the tab's right-click menu or Ctrl+K "Keep this site solid", or list sites in Customize. It needs a window material (not "None").
- **Bookmark manager** (Ctrl+Shift+O, Menu → Manage bookmarks, or the button in the sidebar's Bookmarks header): search, sort by name, address or folder, pick several (Shift/Ctrl+click, Ctrl+A), then open, move to a folder, sort a folder A–Z or remove them. Drag to reorder while it shows your own order. Delete removes, Enter opens, F2 edits, and everything can be undone from the notice.
- **Workspaces**: separate sets of tabs, like Work and Home. Click "Tabs" at the top of the tab list to make one and switch, press Ctrl+Shift+E for the next, or right-click a tab → Move to workspace. Other workspaces keep their pages (and keep playing music) until you come back, and they're restored on the next start. Ctrl+K and the address bar find tabs in every workspace.
- **Mouse gestures**: hold the right button and draw. ← back, → forward, ↑↓ reload, ↓ new tab, ↓→ close tab. A plain right-click still opens the menu. Turn them off in Customize → Layout.
- **Site permissions**: sites ask before using your camera, microphone, location, notifications and the like, in a bar at the top, and you can remember the answer. Customize → Privacy lists what you've allowed or blocked, with "Ask again". Notification requests a page makes without you clicking anything are quietly declined.
- **Tracker blocking level** (Customize → Privacy): Balanced by default, Strict, Basic or Off.
- **Whole-page screenshot** (Ctrl+Shift+X, or Menu): the entire page, not just what's on screen, saved to Pictures\Lilt and copied to the clipboard.
- **The window lifts while you drag it**: its content shrinks a touch and turns slightly see-through, then settles when you let go. Customize → Look → Motion, along with the animation length.
- **Two new themes**: Midnight (true black, quiet sage) and Paper (warm and light, on Mica). **Save your own**: Customize → Look → "Save as preset…" keeps the current look as a one-click preset (right-click one of yours to delete it).
- **Two new home backgrounds**, Aurora and Waves, drawn from your theme's colors, and a Dim slider so a background image stays readable.
- **More layout settings**: tab width in a tab strip, close buttons on every tab (or none), the address bar's width, dark mode for every website, classic scrollbars.

### Better

- Glass is clearer: lighter tint, more see-through surfaces, Aurora on the home tab, and see-through websites on by default in that theme.
- Light has stronger borders and more readable secondary text.

### Protected content (Netflix and the like)

WebView2 supports Microsoft PlayReady, which is what Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and others use in Microsoft Edge, so they should play. Google's Widevine isn't available in WebView2, so a site that only offers Widevine won't play. Hardware-level PlayReady (needed for 4K on some services) can depend on your graphics driver.
