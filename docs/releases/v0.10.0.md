Lilt 0.10 brings an interface size for everything Lilt draws, a log you can read at about:logs, a visualizer that doesn't go blank, and videos that play in the panel without the rest of their page.

**Download `Lilt-v0.10.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### Interface size

- **Customize → Look → Interface size** (75% to 150%, also in Ctrl+K): text, buttons, the toolbar, tabs, the sidebar, the panel, the player, menus, Customize itself, the palette and dialogs grow or shrink together, keeping their proportions. Pages keep their own zoom (Ctrl + and -). The home page and about:logs follow it too.
- **Text size** now reaches all of Lilt, not just the address bar and tab list. Bigger text makes the toolbar, buttons and rows taller so nothing gets cut off.
- **The player grows with its width.** Drag its new corner to resize it, or widen the panel it's docked in. Its artwork, title and buttons scale with it. The visualizer resizes from its corner too.
- Keys for "Make Lilt bigger", "Make Lilt smaller" and "Lilt at 100%" can be set in Customize → Keys. They have none by default, because Ctrl+Alt is AltGr on many keyboards.

### Logs and crashes

- **Type `about:logs` in the address bar** (or Menu → Logs and crashes, or Ctrl+K). This page shows:
  - **Lilt's own log:** each start, errors it recovered from, errors that stopped it, pages and engine processes that crashed or stopped responding, and theme problems.
  - **Windows' crash reports for Lilt:** crashes Windows recorded that Lilt couldn't write down itself, with the part that failed and what the error code means.
  - **The web engine's crash reports.**
- Filter by crashes, errors, warnings or info (`about:crashes` opens on crashes). **Copy** puts the whole log on the clipboard for a bug report.
- If Lilt didn't close properly last time, or hits an error and carries on, its notice now opens this page.
- The page is built from memory for you alone. Web pages can't open it, show it inside themselves or read it, and only the page itself can clear the log.

### Media and the panel

- **The visualizer no longer goes blank.** Lilt used to stop listening to system sound to try the page, then switch back, and showed nothing in between. Now it keeps system sound and, after 1.5 seconds of quiet, listens to the page as well. It draws whichever one has sound.
- **The player follows the track you hear**, not a muted video or a short sound playing beside it.
- **Videos in the panel show only the video.** Pop a video out and drag it onto the panel, or right-click a tab → Move the video to the panel. Whole pages still don't go in.

### Steadier

- Lilt's files (tabs, bookmarks, history, settings) are written to disk in full before they replace the old copy. A file that won't load is kept beside it as `.corrupt` instead of being overwritten on the next save.
- Addresses you type or open from bookmarks lose tracking tags (`utm_…`, `fbclid`, `gclid` and others).
