Lilt 0.5 is a polish release: the bug you found, and a pass over everyday use.

**Download `Lilt-v0.5.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip it over your old copy (or anywhere) and run `Lilt.exe`.** Tabs, bookmarks and settings carry over.

### Fixed

- **Importing a password export no longer creates bookmarks.** Chrome's "Export passwords" file (and Edge, Firefox, Bitwarden or 1Password exports) is recognized and nothing from it is added. If an earlier import already turned it into bookmarks, import the same file again: Lilt finds those bookmarks by their exact name and address and offers to remove them (with Undo). The notice also points you to Proton Pass's import, which is where the passwords belong. Lilt can't put passwords into the engine's own password store, because WebView2 doesn't allow it.
- Minimizing Lilt now always triggers efficiency mode, even when Windows leaves it as the foreground window.

### Easier

- **The address bar completes as you type** ("you" becomes "youtube.com") and lists matching open tabs, bookmarks and pages you've visited. Use Up/Down to pick, Enter to go, Shift+Delete to forget a page, and Ctrl+Enter to turn a word into www.word.com. History stays on your PC (`history.json`), is never kept in InPrivate, can be turned off, and Menu → Clear history clears it.
- **Your search engine:** DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia or Kagi (Customize → Layout, or Ctrl+K "search with"), or any https address with `%s`. The home tab's search box uses it and also accepts addresses.
- **Ctrl+click and middle-click open links in tabs behind the current one**, in order, like other browsers. Middle-clicking a bookmark does the same.
- **Bookmarks:** edit (name, address, folder), copy the address, open it in split view, remove a whole folder. Every removal and every import can be undone from the notice.
- **Tidier address bar:** pages show without `https://` until you click in. http pages still show `http://` and a "Not secure" label.
- Notices close by themselves (info after 6 s, or 15 s if they have a button; not while your pointer is on them). Warnings stay.
- The menu gained Split view, "Find a command, tab or setting" (Ctrl+K) and Clear history. Menu and tooltip shortcuts follow your remapped keys, and so does the home tab's hint.
- Tabs: right-click → Copy address.
- First start greets you with a one-click bookmark import.
